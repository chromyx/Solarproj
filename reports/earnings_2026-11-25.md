## 10. Limitations

- Weather years (2005-2023) and price years (2023-2026) were not observed together, so links between sunny days and low prices are only partly captured.
- Production assumes the plant always runs and the grid never curtails it.
- Assumes all output is sold at the day-ahead price (perfect day-ahead forecast, no imbalance).
- 15.5 MW treated as DC MWp (owner's best guess). If it is AC, energy is higher.
- PVGIS hourly values are hour averages; their timing is approximate (+/- 30 min).
- A bigger plant selling into a thin market can lower midday prices; not modeled.
